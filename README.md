# RETAILPULSE — Sales Performance & Customer Insights Analytics System

## Project Overview

RETAILPULSE is a Python-based sales analytics project designed to analyze retail transaction data and generate useful business insights.

The project follows the workflow:

**DATA → CLEAN → ANALYZE → VISUALIZE → INTERPRET → RECOMMEND**

## Objectives

The project analyzes:

* Sales performance
* Profit performance
* Product performance
* Category performance
* Regional performance
* Customer revenue contribution
* Monthly sales trends

## Business Questions

The analysis answers questions including:

* What is the total revenue?
* What is the total profit?
* Which category generates the most revenue?
* Which category generates the most profit?
* Which region generates the most revenue?
* Which region has the weakest profit performance?
* What are the top 10 products by sales?
* Who are the top 10 customers by revenue?
* Which month has the highest sales?
* Does the highest-sales category also have the highest profit?

## Advanced Analysis

The project also includes a Pareto Analysis to determine the percentage of total revenue contributed by the top 20% of products.
